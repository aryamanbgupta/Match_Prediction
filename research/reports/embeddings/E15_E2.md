## What the ladder has established so far

1. **State features carry ~0.018–0.021 LL** regardless of representation.
2. **The backoff must be learned** (E1.5); mean-vector UNK is actively harmful
   at cricket's 13–23% unknown-player rates.
3. **At equal information, EB shrinkage still beats learned embeddings by
   ~0.013.** Nothing in a plain embedding objective replicates
   sample-size-adaptive pooling.
4. Probes remain at chance and neighbors mostly noise through E2 — context
   improved prediction but did NOT clean the geometry. Style structure does
   not emerge from outcome prediction at this scale/noise level on its own.

All four findings point at the same synthesis: **E4 (embedding = projection
of EB features + learned offset)** — vertical shrinkage from EB, lateral
generalization from the offsets. E3 (time) remains queued; its value-add is
now expected to be modest for LL but relevant for the current-skill probe.
