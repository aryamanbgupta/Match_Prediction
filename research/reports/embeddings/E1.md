## Claim status: SUPPORTED NEGATIVE vs B0b (single seed)

This is a per-ball representation result, not a production or downstream
promotion verdict. On the frozen temporal split, pure-ID embeddings lose to
the EB logistic control overall and on unseen pairs. A repeated-seed estimate
would be required for a precise uncertainty interval, but the observed gap is
large enough to retain the qualitative negative.

| per-ball LL | E1 | B0b (EB logistic) | B0a (prior) |
|---|---|---|---|
| validation | 1.4879 | **1.4691** | 1.4920 |
| val unseen pairs | 1.4921 | **1.4674** | 1.4910 |
| test | 1.4839 | **1.4577** | 1.4807 |
| test unseen pairs | 1.4863 | **1.4538** | 1.4770 |

E1 barely beats the prior on validation and **loses to it on test**. B0c
(production XGBoost) reference: 1.4253.

## Diagnosis (val LL sliced by min(batter,bowler) train balls)

| bucket | n | E1 | B0b | B0a |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unknown (0 balls) | 16,669 | 1.5111 | 1.4620 | 1.4856 |
| 1–199 | 32,742 | 1.4897 | 1.4666 | 1.4923 |
| 200–999 | 41,662 | 1.4813 | 1.4683 | 1.4885 |
| 1000–4999 | 32,411 | 1.4843 | 1.4772 | 1.5000 |
| 5000+ | 808 | 1.4281 | **1.4277** | 1.4707 |

1. **The mean-embedding UNK is actively harmful**: on unknown-player balls
   E1 (1.5111) is far worse than just using the prior (1.4856). 13.4% of
   val balls and 22.8% of test balls involve an unknown player — league
   churn makes the backoff path load-bearing, and ours was a crutch.
2. **No shrinkage → noise vectors everywhere below ~5000 balls.** E1 only
   catches B0b in the tiny 5000+ bucket. EB's Dirichlet shrinkage wins
   every other bucket. Nothing in E1's objective forces lateral pooling.
3. **Probes at chance**: bat_hand 0.709 vs majority 0.711; bowl_kind 0.321
   vs 0.321; bowl_arm 0.626 vs 0.633. The vectors encode outcome-rate
   noise, not player attributes.
4. **Neighbors mostly noise, faint real structure**: the WI power-hitting
   cluster (Pollard–Bravo–Narine–Hetmyer) and spinner groupings (Chahal→
   Jadeja/Harbhajan) do appear — note the likely **teammate/league
   confound** (co-occurrence drives similarity without context features).
5. Overfit signature: train LL 1.43 vs val 1.488 at the stop epoch.

## What this rung taught (the ladder working as designed)

The pre-registered prediction held: raw embeddings butcher the rare-player
tail, and in T20 the "tail" is most of the distribution (59–70% unseen
pairs, 13–23% unseen players). Identity signal exists (~0.023 LL per B0b)
but extracting it with embeddings requires an explicit backoff/shrinkage
mechanism — which is precisely what E4's EB-anchoring provides. This is
the motivation-by-measurement for the paper's core design.

## Next rung options

- **E1.5 (recommended)**: one change — make the backoff *learned*.
  Identity dropout (p≈0.05 → trained UNK vector) + stronger weight decay
  on embedding tables. Directly targets failures 1–2; minutes to run.
- E2 (context) per original plan — won't fix the tail, but deconfounds.
- Skip to E4 (EB anchoring) — strongest fix, but skips ladder steps.
