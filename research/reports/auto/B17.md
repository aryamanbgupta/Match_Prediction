## Reading

1. **The bias is carried run mass, and the graft is the biggest single
   piece — but the sign flip is the 6-class channel.** `C_extras`
   (−0.039559: the flat graft carries 0.0200 vs 0.059559 actual extras mass)
   and `C_fold` (−0.005300: threes credited as 2) are algebraically
   model-independent — both stacks pay them equally. The entire i7−legacy
   contrast is `C_class`: the legacy calibrated venue_on path **over-carries
   +0.062025** through its 6-class head, masking the graft deficit (net
   +0.017); the no-weights i7 head is nearly marginally neutral (−0.007926),
   so the graft deficit shows through almost in full (net −0.053 ≈ the
   observed quote bias).
2. **This retro-explains D3.** D3's empirical-rates graft FAILED its guard
   gate on legacy by over-shooting innings totals — true-rate extras were
   being added on top of a +0.062 over-carry. On i7 that over-carry is gone,
   so the same fix should now close most of the gap without D3's failure
   mode. That falsifiable claim is B18.
3. **Side finding (recorded, NOT queued):** the legacy over-carry is a
   serving-path artifact — under `venue_zero` (the arm the E5 calibrator was
   fit on) the calibrated legacy stack reproduces val marginals EXACTLY (all
   six per-class deltas 0.00000); the deployed venue_on arm carries
   +0.065392 more. i7 is venue-insensitive (−0.001835). Same family as the
   D6 side finding (B7 already showed a venue_on refit degrades tails);
   legacy is retired to replay — no queue entry.
4. **Side finding 2 (recorded, NOT queued):** both stacks share a
   score-tercile gradient (bias falls as checkpoint score rises; cp6 T3: i7
   −11.20, legacy −1.12) — the continuation under-responds to the innings'
   own in-progress scoring rate on both engines. Orthogonal to the mass
   channel; quote-layer analytics material.
