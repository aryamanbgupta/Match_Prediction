# D9 — Decayed margin-aware team-results ELO (replacement test for win_rate features)

**Verdict: FAILED** (2026-07-20; both arms miss both pre-committed floors vs the
same-session base control)

## Hypothesis

`win_rate_diff` (crude last-10) is the model's highest-gain feature; the direct
team-result family is its best signal but poorest-estimated one. A time-decayed,
margin-aware team ELO updated on match results is a strictly richer estimator of
the same construct, match-level by nature (immune to the lineup-aggregation
collapse that killed M3–M5). Framed as REPLACEMENT as well as ADD because it was
expected to fail the |r|>0.5 redundancy check vs `win_rate_diff`.

## Reading

The hypothesis' premise was *confirmed* pre-training — team-results ELO is a
genuinely better univariate predictor of the winner than `win_rate_diff` (T up
to 0.206 vs 0.155, margin-aware variants best) — and it still bought nothing in
the full model. The marginal information in a results-ELO *conditioned on the
other 48 features* is tiny: the top6/bottom5 player-ELO aggregates already carry
team strength through a richer channel (lineup composition, updated per match),
and depth-4 XGB combines them with `win_rate_diff` well enough that a sharper
scalar of the same construct adds ~nothing. Notably REPLACE is strictly worse
than base (ΔLL +0.0017, ΔROI −1.41pp): the crude last-10 window carries real
incremental signal the smooth ELO doesn't (bounded-window recency reacts faster
than K-limited updates). This is the 6th match-level feature direction to die
(M3, M4, M5, M6, A5/A6, D9) — and the first to die *after* passing the
correlation check, which sharpens the lesson: univariate target-correlation
premium over an existing feature does not survive conditioning on the full
feature set. The additive-feature frontier at match level looks closed at both
gates now; remaining match-level levers are training-procedure (D7 landed) and
betting-layer (A7 landed) classes.

## What I'd try next

Nothing in this direction. If the team-results construct is ever revisited, the
one framing not tested here is ELO as a *prior* for the win_rate features
(shrink last-10 toward the ELO expectation instead of 0.5) — but given ADD
couldn't clear a quarter of the floor, the expected value is low; I did not
queue it. No new PENDING ideas appended (D10/D11 precedent — nothing genuinely
promising surfaced).
