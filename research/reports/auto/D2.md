## Hypothesis

`MatchState.update()` rotated strike on ANY odd `runs` (`sim_v1_2.py:331-332`
pre-fix), and `process_ball` gives WIDE/NO_BALL `runs=1` — so the single
extra run of a wide/no-ball wrongly swapped the striker on ~2% of deliveries.
Balls-faced excluded only WIDE, so a NO_BALL charged the striker a ball faced
and credited the extra run to their tally. Both misattribute batter exposure
and bias batter-level props at the margin.

## What was done

`scripts/sim_v1_2.py`, `MatchState.update()` only (2 hunks, commit `46f26dc`):

1. **Batsman stats**: runs + balls-faced now credited only on legal
   deliveries — `outcome not in [WIDE, NO_BALL]` (was: `!= WIDE`).
2. **Strike rotation**: rotates only on odd off-the-bat runs —
   `outcome not in [WIDE, NO_BALL] and runs % 2 == 1` (was: any odd runs).

Not touched: over accounting (extras already correctly excluded from legal
ball count), end-of-over rotation, wicket replacement, `run_rate` (D1),
team score (extras still count to the team).

**Unit check** (`scripts/auto/d2_unit_check.py`, pre-committed): 26/26
assertions on a scripted over — extras never rotate strike nor charge the
striker; odd off-the-bat runs rotate; wicket replacement, over accounting,
team-score-with-extras all hold. Re-verified pass at eval time (2026-07-20).

## Reading: why does a provably-correct fix regress a guard?

The most likely mechanism is the pre-existing bug the D2 unit check itself
surfaced (out of D2's scope): `_simulate_innings` builds its per-ball
batting card keyed on `state.striker_idx` AFTER `update()` has already
rotated strike / replaced a dismissed batter (`sim_v1_2.py:3558` BallResult,
`:3567` card) — a ONE scored by batter 0 is carded to batter 1, and a WICKET
ball cards a ball-faced to the INCOMING batter. The internal `batsman_stats`
(used for crease features) credits correctly; the **card** is what
batter-level prop extraction reads. Under the old rotation semantics the two
attribution errors partially overlapped; fixing rotation alone re-scrambles
the card mismatch rather than removing it, and shifts which batter faces
early deliveries (first-over composition) — consistent with a small
first-over MAE regression and an otherwise null scan. The two bugs are
entangled and should be fixed and gated **as a unit** — appended as **D14**.

TABLED (not FAILED) is the right bucket: the semantics are objectively
correct (26/26 scripted-over assertions) and the fix is raw material for the
D14 combination; it just must not ship alone under the current
card-extraction path.

## Operational notes

The n=261 eval was launched EIGHT times. Launches 1–7 (2026-07-20 02:45 →
07:00Z) all died right after StatsProvider startup with no traceback: each
relay iteration launched the eval (nohup-detached or background-task) and
then ended its turn — the session closed and the child was reaped. The ~2-min
spacing of launches 4–7 is the loop runner cycling iterations, each
relaunching and dying the same way. Launch 8 (07:02:50Z, this iteration)
succeeded because the session **blocked synchronously** on the run —
foreground polling loops in ≤10-min windows until the detail JSON appeared
(2230.4 s total). Lesson for future iterations: for any eval longer than the
turn, do not end the turn until the artifact exists; background/nohup
launches do not survive session close.
