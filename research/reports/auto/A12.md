# A12 — Dew as a ball-level second-innings covariate (A6 follow-up)

**Verdict: FAILED** (2026-07-11). Sim-gate pair. GATE 1 does not improve
(pooled CI includes 0) → FAILED regardless of GATE 2. Implementation reverted
(`eabd701` reverts `834909c`); default sim path byte-unchanged.

## Hypothesis

A6 showed evening humidity (a dew proxy) carries **no match-level winner
signal** (|r_target| ≈ 0.005). But the physics — wet ball in the chase, harder
to grip/field — is fundamentally a **ball-level, second-innings** effect that
match-level aggregation washes out. A dew covariate feeding the sim's
second-innings scoring/wicket rates is a cleaner test of the same physics, and
it lives in the sim/prop gate, not the winner gate.

## What I did

Reused the A6 open-meteo weather cache (`data/external/weather/`, evening RH per
venue/date; no new network). Built `DewVectorScalingCalibrator`
(`scripts/auto/a12_fit_dew_calibrator.py`), a **centered** dew-tilt on top of
the E5 v1 global vector:

- Fit two marginal-matching vectors on validation **innings-2** balls — one on
  low-dew balls, one on high-dew balls (split at the fit-time median evening
  RH = 68.0%).
- `tilt = v_high / v_low`; store `sqrt_tilt`. Applied at eval keyed by
  `(innings, venue, match_date)`: innings-2 high-dew → `global · sqrt_tilt`,
  innings-2 low-dew → `global / sqrt_tilt`, everything else → `global` (==
  E5 v1 **exactly**). The ±sqrt split is centered so at mean dew the correction
  equals the vec baseline — isolating the dew *differential* from mere
  innings-2 re-conditioning.
- Wired a `dew_aware` dispatch into `sim_v1_2.predict_next_ball` that passes
  `state` (innings/venue/date) only for this calibrator; every other path
  (default vec, A14 over-aware) stays byte-for-byte unchanged.

**Dew signal strength (fitted tilt):** max |tilt − 1| = **0.066** (wicket, hi/lo
= 1.066; applied sqrt = 1.033). Every other class within ±2% (tilt ≈ 1.00). So
the strongest per-ball correction is a ~3.3% wicket tilt in high dew; all others
< 1%. Eval coverage 96.6% of innings-2 balls (446,669 (venue,date) RH rows;
uncovered venues/dates fall back to the global vector).

Ran the dew calibrator through recipe B (`prop_backtest.py`, n=261 × 100 sims,
seed 42, `--ball-calibrator vector` path) → `models/auto/a12/detail_dew_n261.json`.
Paired it against the E5 v1 single-vector baseline
(`models/auto/a8/detail_vec_n261.json`, same seed/matches) with
`scripts/auto/a12_gate_analysis.py` (cluster-bootstrap by match, 2000 reps).

**Pairing sanity check** (built into the harness): `n_changed` ≈ half of
`n_all` on every innings-2 family (e.g. innings_runs 210/522, bowler_wkts
1208/3107) — i.e. only innings-2 obs differ, innings-1 obs are byte-identical to
the vec baseline. Design validated; the deltas below are pure dew effect.

## Verdict

**FAILED.** GATE 1 (2nd-innings scoring + wicket calibration) pooled dBrier
−0.0003 with CI [−0.0013, +0.0006] **includes 0** — not the A14 bar (CI
excluding 0) for a real, above-noise gain. The scoring point-estimate is even
slightly *worse* (+0.0003); the wicket/econ side is a directionless −0.0004.
GATE 1 does not improve → FAILED (GATE 2 is moot, but note it did not hold
cleanly either: `top_bowler` regressed a hair, CI [+0.0001, +0.0005] excluding
0 — the innings-2 wicket tilt slightly miscalibrates bowler-of-the-match).

This is the **third** negative on the same dew physics, now at ball resolution:
A6 (match-level winner, null), and now A12 (ball-level 2nd-innings, null). It
also reproduces the **A8 washout mechanism** exactly: A8 found 3–7% per-ball
phase tilts net to zero at prop aggregation; A12's dew tilt is *weaker still*
(≤3.3%, mostly <1%), so it was never going to move a multi-ball prop's Brier.
Dew is real physics, but at T20 ball level its residual — after the model
already sees venue/chase context, batter/bowler form, and match state — is below
the noise floor of a 261×100 prop backtest.

## What I'd try next

The dew lever is exhausted at both match and ball resolution; do not re-open it
without a fundamentally stronger signal (e.g. per-ball *measured* dew/ball-change
events from ball-by-ball commentary, not a venue-date humidity proxy — not
available in this repo). The remaining sim-gate frontier is **not** marginal-rate
scaling at all: A8 (phase), A12 (dew), and even A14 (per-over, which only won on
the single, non-washing `team_first_over_mae`) all confirm that any linear
per-ball probability tilt washes out on aggregated props. The open, distinct
lever is **A13** (sim *dispersion* calibration — the vec baseline under-disperses
score totals, P10–P90 coverage 53–74% vs ideal 80%), which is a variance defect
orthogonal to everything the marginal calibrators touch. That is the idea most
likely to move the aggregated tail-Brier props A8/A12 could not. Recommend A13
next (or the A15 parsimony check on the one thing that *did* land, A14).
