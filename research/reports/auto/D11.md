**Verdict: FAILED** (pre-committed eval-only gate on the primary arm: neither

## Hypothesis

D7's train-time swap augmentation makes the match model *approximately*
antisymmetric but not exactly; averaging the two orientations at predict time
(`p_sym = (p(A,B) + 1 − p(B,A)) / 2`) enforces exact antisymmetry and should be
a free eval-only variance cut on top of D7 (flagged in D7's own gate note).

  - **d7_swap** (PRIMARY, gated): `models/auto/d7/swap_seed*` on v2_clean.

  - Raw re-scoring reproduces the saved `test_predictions.json` **exactly**

    last-occurrence rows — the rows the JSON stores and the eval consumes —

## Verdict

Pre-committed gate (primary = d7_swap, eval-only floors): LL not improved
(+0.0013, wrong direction on 4/5 seeds), ROI not improved (−0.83pp). **Neither
→ FAILED.** Eval-only: no production code was touched, nothing to revert;
analysis harness kept (A9/A11 precedent).

## Reading

- **On swap-trained models there is nothing left for inference-time averaging
  to collect.** Training-time augmentation already halves residual orientation
  noise (mean|asym| 0.052 base → 0.023 swap-trained → 0.018 on the production
  frame), and averaging away what remains moves LL slightly the *wrong* way on
  4/5 seeds (sub-floor, so formally noise — but consistently unfavorable, and
  ROI drops on the two seeds where LL degrades most). D7's gate-note hypothesis
  resolves negative: the augmentation captures the symmetry benefit in full.
- **The base-arm decomposition is the interesting by-product**: symmetrizing
  the *non-augmented* models recovers essentially all of D7's ROI gain
  (+3.03pp vs D7's +3.01pp; ≥$100k +4.73pp) but only ~10–15% of its LL gain
  (−0.0011 vs D7's −0.0121). D7's ROI arm is antisymmetry enforcement
  (achievable at inference); its LL arm needs the training-time data doubling.
  Since base+sym (LL 0.6307) is still strictly dominated by swap-trained
  (0.6196) at equal ROI, this is mechanism knowledge, not a production lever.
