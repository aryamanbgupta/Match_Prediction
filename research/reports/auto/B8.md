## Reading

Two real findings despite (because of) the null:

1. **The B7 decomposition is confirmed on the regression side.** With the
   refit global dropped, B7's CI-clean regressions vanish exactly: pooled
   tail +0.0079 → +0.0005; bowler_wkts_1plus +0.0024 → −0.0005. The harm
   in B7's refit stack was entirely the global vector, as B8's hypothesis
   asserted.
2. **The over-0 gain no longer exists on the current engine.** The blind
   `team_first_over_mae` baseline drifted from 3.535/3.526 (A8/A15 era,
   pre-D14) to 3.411 on the b10 blind twin. D14's extraction-window fix
   (over labels pre-increment — the old code rolled each over's 6th legal
   delivery into the next over, exactly the first-over window A15's vector
   was compensating) and D15's run-out channel absorbed what the over-0
   calibrator was buying. A stale-but-correctly-applied over-0 vector
   (application verified bit-exact, max prob shift 8.4e-2 on over-0 balls)
   now nets to nothing on any of 32 families.

**Consequence:** the first-over calibrator lever is CLOSED on the current
engine. A15's artifact is superseded-by-drift; bare v1 remains the sole
default calibrator; do not re-run or combine B8 unless the sim engine
changes again in a way that plausibly re-opens a first-over marginal
defect. The canonical seed-43 baseline remains
`models/auto/b10/detail_blind_s43_n261.json` (B8 shipped nothing, no
re-baseline).
