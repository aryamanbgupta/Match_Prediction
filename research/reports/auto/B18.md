## Hypothesis

B17 attributed the i7 quote-path P50 under-prediction (−4.78/−3.03/−1.95
remaining runs at checkpoints 6/10/15) to carried run mass: g_i7 = −0.0528
runs/legal ball, of which −0.0396 is the flat 1%+1% extras graft
under-carrying explicit extras (0.0200 grafted vs 0.059559 actual). D3's
empirical-rates graft FAILED on the legacy stack because that path
over-carries +0.0620 through the 6-class channel; the promoted no-weights i7
head is marginally neutral, so the same fix should close most of the gap
WITHOUT the innings-total regression. That was the falsifiable claim, and it
held.
