# B16 — Quote-layer coverage re-check on the promoted i7 stack

**Verdict: LANDED** (2026-08-03). Both pre-committed gates met; certified
arm = **B15 scales**. The headline finding is a refutation of the idea's own
premise: the B15 scale-only quote calibrator is **not** stale for the i7
path — it carries over as the quote calibrator of record for both stacks.

## Hypothesis

B15's scale-only quote calibrator (scales cp6/cp10/cp15 = 1.19/1.09/1.26,
shift 0) was fit against the legacy balanced-weights engine's
under-dispersion. A13's STEP 0 showed the promoted i7 no-weights stack
disperses materially wider at the *prop* layer (first_over P10–P90 coverage
64.6→77.2%, batter_runs 73.8→82.5%), so on an i7 quote-path migration the
B15 scales would plausibly over-widen bands through the 0.90 ceiling, and
raw i7 quotes might already be in band with no calibrator (a D17-style null
one layer up).

## Reading

1. **The premise did not reproduce at the quote layer.** A13's prop-layer
   dispersion widening (full-innings sampled totals) does not propagate to
   conditional mid-innings continuations: raw band widths are essentially
   unchanged vs legacy and the cp15 (death-overs, short-horizon)
   under-dispersion defect reproduces on i7 (0.684 vs legacy 0.660). The
   feared over-widening through the 0.90 ceiling also did not happen (max
   corrected coverage 0.838). Conclusion: the B15 scales are engine-robust,
   not legacy-specific — `models/auto/b15/quote_calibrator_scale_only.json`
   is the quote calibrator of record for **both** the legacy and i7 quote
   paths.
2. **New fact — the raw P50 bias sign-flips on i7.** Legacy over-predicted
   remaining runs (+4.259/+2.777/+0.410 at cp6/10/15); the promoted stack
   under-predicts (−4.781/−3.026/−1.946), ≈ −0.34 runs/over at cp6 — the
   mirror image of B5's +0.33/over. This re-confirms the B15 diagnostic
   (never fit a location/shift term from another era or stack) from the
   opposite direction, and is queued for attribution as B17 (the D3
   extras-graft composition is the prime suspect).
3. Operational: the i7 quote run is 1.65× faster than legacy (898.8 s vs
   1487.2 s) — in-play quote experiments are now cheaper.
