## Honest caveats

1. **Val/test bias sign mismatch.** Fitted shifts are negative (sim
   under-predicts remaining runs on the 2024-12→2025-06 val pool) while raw
   test bias is positive (+4.670/+3.204/+0.514 on the 2025-07→2026-04 test
   pool). The shift term therefore moves test P50 the *wrong* way (corrected
   test bias +6.118/+4.985/+3.485) and every corrected test MAE is slightly
   worse than raw. Essentially all of B14's value is the scale (band-widening)
   term. A scale-only variant was deliberately NOT run against the frozen test
   set — that would be post-hoc selection.
2. **cp15 per-cp dMAE CI straddles zero post-correction** ([−2.565, +0.461];
   raw was [−2.613, −0.004], itself barely clean). The gate passes on the
   pooled CI as pre-committed; do not quote cp15 alone as a CI-clean win.
3. **Price paid:** pooled dMAE margin −3.086 → −2.774 (~0.31) for the cp15
   coverage fix — explicit and modest.
4. Val skip rate 6.06% (2× the test precedent 3.07%) but under the 10% abort
   bar and fully structural.
