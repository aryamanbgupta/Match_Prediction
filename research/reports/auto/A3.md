# A3 — Direct + v7 sim blend, fine w-sweep

**Status:** FAILED (2026-07-11)
**Type:** Pure eval composition — no training, no source-code change.

## Hypothesis
A small v7-sim weight (w ∈ 0.05–0.3) adds ball-level information the direct
match-level model lacks, helping the close-match slice without hurting the
≥$50k headline.

## Method
Logit-space blend `logit(P_final) = w·logit(P_sim) + (1−w)·logit(P_direct)`
between the production direct predictions
(`models/xgb_match_v3_m7_production/test_predictions.json`) and the existing
v7 sim envelope (`eval_out_phase5_hier/hier_all_20260425_165622.json`), swept
at w ∈ {0.00, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30}, then resliced against
`betting_odds_polymarket.json`. Baseline is w=0.00 in the same session (same
direct predictions, same envelope, same 6 passthrough matches), so seed
variance is fully removed and the eval-only noise floor applies
(ΔLL < 0.002 / ΔROI < 2pp). Gate decision on ≥$50k only; close-match slice
computed for information (avoid slice-shopping).

## Verdict
**FAILED.** LL degrades **monotonically** with any positive sim weight on
every slice — the sim strictly drags the direct model's calibration the wrong
way. ROI is noisy around the w=0 value and never clears the 2pp floor (best
case w=0.20 at +0.31pp, with LL +0.0097 worse — well past the 0.002 floor).
There is no w where LL improves, so at most one metric could ever move, and
even that one doesn't clear noise. Neither gate metric improves → FAILED. The
close-match slice — the specific thing the hypothesis hoped to help — actually
gets *worse* at every w. This confirms the CLAUDE.md finding that v7 sim loses
the winner-market race by ~0.07 LL: it carries no winner-market information the
direct model lacks, so mixing it in is pure noise injection.

## What I'd try next
The sim envelope in its current form is a dead end for winner-market blending —
its probabilities are strictly less informative than the direct model's, so any
mixing weight is a loss. If ball-level information is ever going to help the
winner market, it has to enter as *features into the direct model* (e.g. a
sim-derived scalar like simulated P(win) or projected margin distribution as a
single input column, letting the tree learn where/whether to trust it), not as
a post-hoc probability average. That is a training-side idea (re-materialize +
retrain), not an eval composition, and would need the correlation-check
discipline against the existing 49 features first. Filed as A10.
