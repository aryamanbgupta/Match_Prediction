## Hypothesis

Every sim model wrapper grafts a flat 1% wide + 1% no-ball onto the 6-class
model output AFTER the calibrator runs, then renormalizes — and the rates are
wrong: real T20 wides ≈3.5–4% per delivery, no-balls ≈0.5%, so the flat graft
under-produces wides ~4× and over-produces no-balls ~2×. Extras are also baked
into training labels (a wide-1 is labeled `one`), so the graft double-counts;
that label-side rework needs `parsing_v2.py` (loop-forbidden — Interactive
backlog I5). This idea is the sim-side half only: empirical rates + a
composition that preserves the calibrated 6-class relative marginals exactly.

## Verdict

**TABLED** — exactly one gate met, per the pre-committed mapping. Reverted the
sim change (`87f4fe6`; `sim_v1_2.py` byte-identical to the pre-D3 head, git
diff vs `3667a29` = 0 lines). Harness (`d3_build_extras_rates.py`,
`d3_unit_check.py`, `d3_gate_analysis.py`) + `models/auto/d3/` artifacts kept.
**D15 baseline remains canonical** — no re-baseline occurred.

**Reading:** the pre-run mechanism note called the failure mode exactly.
Training labels already fold wide runs into the 6 classes (a wide-1 is labeled
`one` — I5), so the per-legal-ball model output is already contaminated with
extras runs; grafting the TRUE empirical extras mass on top double-counts.
Total extras mass 1.96% → 4.21% per delivery adds ~+2.9 extras runs/innings
(120 × p/(1−p): 2.4 → 5.3), inflating simulated totals — and the innings-runs
O/U Briers regress CI-clean at every line while shorter-horizon totals
(pp_total) only lean worse. The `team_total_sixes_mae` regression is
consistent second-order: inflated extras shorten chases (target reached in
fewer legal balls → fewer boundary events carded in innings 2). The old flat
1%+1% graft was never "right" — it was an accidental partial compensation for
the label contamination, and replacing it with the truth exposes the
double-count. Rate realism alone bought nothing observable: no prop family
improved CI-clean (top_bowler −0.0006 and highest_over −0.0054 lean favorable
but straddle 0).

## What I'd try next

The sim-side lever is blocked on the label side: **I5** (train the ball model
on off-the-bat runs, exclude wide/no-ball rows) is the real fix, and D3 now
gives it a quantified acceptance check — after I5, re-apply this exact graft
(harness kept for that purpose) and the innings-total regression should
disappear by construction, since the 6-class block would then carry zero
extras mass. Keep D3 TABLED as the sim-side half of that unit. A "residual
mass" stopgap (tune graft rates to innings-total calibration instead of
empirical delivery rates) was considered and rejected: it abandons rate
realism — the only thing D3 verifiably won — to recover what the flat graft
already approximates, and it would fail D3's own GATE 1 by construction. No
new PENDING ideas appended (D5/D6/B10 already cover the live sim levers; I5
is interactive).
