(+0.0027 [+0.0007,+0.0050]) while `team_first_over_mae` improved CI-clean
(−0.132) and correctness was proven. Mechanism: correct over-final-ball

missing run-out channel partially cancelled; applying D2 + D14 + D4 as ONE
unit should keep D14's wins while the run-out channel removes the unmasked
wicket overshoot.

## Reading

- **The entanglement hypothesis held.** D14 alone: 1plus +0.0027
  CI-clean worse, 2plus +0.0022 CI-clean worse. With the run-out channel:
  2plus flips to CI-clean BETTER (−0.0054), 3plus CI-clean better
  (−0.0013), 1plus no longer CI-clean worse. Removing ~7.5% of
  bowler-credited wickets bites hardest on the multi-wicket tail — the
  direction D4 predicted.
- **Honest caveat on P1:** the 1plus point estimate (+0.0047) is not
  better than D14's (+0.0027); its CI (wider here) includes 0 by a hair

  met — but a residual 1plus overshoot plausibly remains (E5-residual
  class-weight bias and/or D5's ineligible-bowler dilution; both already
  queued as D6/D5). Do not read D15 as "bowler_wkts_1plus fixed".
- `team_first_over_mae` −0.111 confirms D14's extraction-window win
  (−0.132) on an independent draw of the same seed pair — ~6× A15's
  calibrator gain, from correct labels rather than calibration.

  (~1.2% relative) is the one CI-clean negative in the scan; likely the
  D2 rotation change shifting who faces balls. Context-only under the
