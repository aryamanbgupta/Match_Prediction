# A2 — Seed bagging (5-seed probability ensemble)

**Status: FAILED** (neither gate metric improved beyond the noise floor)
**Run:** 2026-07-11 (overnight auto loop)

## Hypothesis
Averaging predicted probabilities across A1's five seed retrains removes seed
variance and improves both LL and ROI — variance reduction with no new
information, historically the safest kind of win.

## What was done
- Averaged `p_team1` (arithmetic mean, probability space) across the five A1
  seed models (`models/auto/a1_seed{29,7,13,42,101}`) into one prediction set
  `models/auto/a2/test_predictions.json` (782 matches; all 5 files verified to
  share identical match keys and metadata).
- Recipe A steps 2–3: `blend_eval_json.py --w 0.0` → `reslice_eval_json.py`
  vs `betting_odds_polymarket.json`. Read the ≥$50k slice.

## Results (≥$50k slice, 168 bets)

| metric | A2 bag | A1 baseline mean (5 seeds) | Δ | noise floor | improved? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Avg Log Loss | **0.6308** | 0.6318 | +0.0010 | 0.007 (1 seed-std) | no (within noise) |
| Flat ROI | **+18.23%** | +20.56% | −2.33pp | 2.3pp | no (declined) |

- A2 ≥$50k: LL 0.6308 [95% CI 0.6004, 0.6612]; Flat ROI +18.23% [95% CI
  −3.07%, +38.79%]; win rate 50.6%. Market LL reference 0.6267.
- Context (not the gate): all-slice LL 0.6221 / ROI +16.65% (n=255);
  ≥$100k LL 0.5903 / ROI +23.96% (n=110).

## Verdict
**FAILED.** LL moved +0.0010 (well inside the 0.007 seed-std floor) and ROI
fell 2.33pp. Neither gate metric improved → FAILED per the dual-metric rule.
Implementation reverted (commit d994bbd); report + log kept.

## Interpretation
- Bagging behaved exactly as the math predicts on **LL**: averaging
  probabilities lands the bagged log loss (0.6308) just below the mean of the
  per-seed log losses (0.6318) — the small Jensen gain — but that gain is an
  order of magnitude smaller than the seed spread it was meant to erase, so it
  is invisible against the noise floor. The best single seed (29: 0.6231) still
  beats the bag; bagging pulled toward the *mean*, not toward the best.
- Bagging did **not** help **ROI**, and here it can't reliably: ROI is a
  discontinuous, threshold function of the predictions (a bet flips on/off at
  the edge=0 boundary and pays the odds). Averaging five prob sets doesn't
  average five ROIs — it produces a new bet-selection that landed at the low
  end of the seed ROI range (+18.23% vs a seed span of +18.2 to +24.0). There
  is no variance-reduction guarantee for a quantity that isn't a smooth mean of
  the inputs.

## What I'd try next
The LL Jensen gain is real but tiny because the five seeds are highly
correlated (same config, same data, only the RNG differs) — bagging correlated
learners barely reduces variance. A2 as-is is dead, but two adjacent moves
could carry more signal: (1) **logit-space averaging** instead of
probability-space — sometimes tightens the tails differently and is worth a
one-command A/B since the seed files are still on disk; (2) bag across
genuinely *decorrelated* learners rather than seeds — e.g. average the M7 model
with A4's regularized logistic stack (different bias class), where the whole
point is that variance reduction needs uncorrelated errors. File the logit
variant as a possible future combination rather than a standalone; the seed
route has shown its ceiling.
