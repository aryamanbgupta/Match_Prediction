## Reading

This is the loop's first validated in-play skill claim: the sim's P50
remaining-runs quote beats naive run-rate extrapolation CI-clean at every
checkpoint, with the margin largest early (−5.0 runs of MAE at over 6) and
monotonically shrinking as the remaining horizon collapses. The failure is
isolated and mechanistically familiar: the cp-15 P10–P90 band is too
*narrow* (width 29.5 vs actual sd 16.9; a calibrated ~2.56σ band would be
~43), i.e. the sim under-disperses short-horizon totals — the same
under-dispersion defect A13 targets at the engine level. Two context signals
worth keeping: (1) coverage is fine at cps 6/10, so the dispersion deficit
concentrates in the death-overs horizon; (2) the P50 overshoots remaining
runs by +4.670/+3.204/+0.514 across cps ≈ +0.33 runs per remaining over,
sign-consistent with the extras-mass double-count D3 documented (speculative
— not established causally here).
