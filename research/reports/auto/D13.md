only −0.0026 incremental on top of swap (combo vs base −0.0147 ≈ swap's
−0.0121 + 0.0026). Both mechanisms apparently harvest much of the same
variance. Meanwhile decay's ROI drag *amplifies* under swap (−1.46pp alone
→ −3.67pp on the swap arm, down 4/5 seeds) — third confirmation (A4, D8,
D13) that LL sharpening decouples from threshold-function flat ROI on this

produced — mean LL 0.6171, **all five seeds beat the market's 0.6267**
(min 0.6154, LL seed-std down to 0.0017) — it just doesn't pay at the
betting layer, and the loop's dual gate is the point.
