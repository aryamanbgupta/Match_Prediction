# A1 — Fresh baseline + seed-variance floor

**Status: LANDED** (instrumentation idea — gate was "5 evals complete")
**Run:** 2026-07-11, supervised dry-run iteration (executed by the session
agent to validate the protocol; commands identical to what overnight agents
run).

## What was done
- Added `--seed` flag to `scripts/xgboost_match_v1.py` (default 29 =
  production value; trainer is editable per program.md).
- Retrained the M7 production config (trainer defaults) with seeds
  {29, 7, 13, 42, 101} → `models/auto/a1_seed<s>/`.
- Evaluated each via recipe A (blend `--w 0.0` → reslice vs
  `betting_odds_polymarket.json`), ≥$50k slice (170 matches, 168 bets).

## Results (≥$50k slice)

| seed | LL | Flat ROI | ROI 95% CI | win rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | 0.6231 | +23.96% | [+2.97, +45.70] | 53.6% |
| 7 | 0.6373 | +18.22% | [−2.30, +40.61] | 50.0% |
| 13 | 0.6293 | +21.30% | [+0.69, +43.63] | 51.8% |
| 42 | 0.6292 | +18.73% | [−2.64, +39.54] | 50.6% |
| 101 | 0.6399 | +20.61% | [+0.11, +42.82] | 51.8% |

**Fresh baseline (mean of 5): LL 0.6318, ROI +20.56%.**
**Seed noise floor: LL std ≈ 0.0068 (range 0.0168), ROI std ≈ 2.3pp (range 5.7pp).**
Market reference LL: 0.6267.

## Notes
- Confirms the E-series "seed luck" finding quantitatively. The historical M7
  headline (LL 0.6299 / ROI +21.90%) sits comfortably inside the seed
  distribution; the seed-29 retrain here landed at LL 0.6231 / +23.96%
  (env/version nondeterminism — do not treat either as special).
- Consequence for verdicts: retrain-based ideas must beat the baseline
  **mean** by more than ~1 seed-std (ΔLL > 0.007, ΔROI > 2.3pp) to count a
  metric as improved. program.md noise qualifier updated accordingly.
- ROI CIs straddle zero for 2 of 5 seeds — single-seed ROI claims on this
  slice are weak evidence in general.

## What I'd try next
A2 (seed bagging) is the direct exploit: averaging these five prediction sets
should recover most of the seed variance for free. The five model dirs are
left in place (`models/auto/a1_seed*`) for A2 to reuse.
