## Hypothesis

mismatch: its P50 shifts are negative (fit on the 2024-12→2025-06 val pool)

(shift ≡ 0, same scales, no refit) should keep the cp15 coverage fix at ~zero
MAE cost. Because that decomposition was learned from the frozen s43 quotes,
the test required fresh Monte Carlo draws (B1→B6 precedent).

## Reading

Scale-only leaves P50 untouched (its corrected MAE/bias rows are identically
the raw rows), so the calibrator's entire contribution is the coverage fix at
exactly zero MAE cost — the B15 hypothesis, confirmed without post-hoc
selection. B14's −0.31 pooled MAE price was pure shift-term damage. The run
also independently re-validates B5's GATE 1 on fresh draws: the raw sim P50
beats naive CI-clean at seed 45. Lesson recorded: never fit a shift/bias term
from a temporally disjoint val pool — the sim's scoring-pace error drifts sign
across eras; scale/dispersion terms transfer, location terms do not.
