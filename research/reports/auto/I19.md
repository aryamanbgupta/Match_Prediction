## Hypothesis

The successor line's training frame `data/xgb_match_data_i7` predates I15/I16:
its `match_id` is the legacy `date_team1_team2_venue` display string and it
lacks the `display_match_id` / `match_identity_version` / `elo_update_version`
columns the current materializer emits. I18 proved the features bit-identical
across that drift, so producing a coherent cricsheet-primary twin
(`data/xgb_match_data_i7_v2`) and proving retrains on it reproduce the I17
arms exactly is pure identity hygiene that pre-clears the frame question in
the human promotion bundle.

## Orchestrator ruling on gate 3

The plan (orchestrator-authored) mis-specified a sub-assertion:
`display_match_id` unique per row. It failed (train 7943/7972, val 522/528,
test 788/798, golden 222/227) — but `scripts/match_identity.py`'s own
docstring defines the display string as "not unique for same-day
doubleheaders and must never be used as a new primary key," and the
executor's read-only diagnostic verified every collision is a genuine
same-day doubleheader (e.g. `1207081`/`1207082` → same Spain–Germany
Almeria display string). The assertion contradicted the contract it was
meant to verify; the primary-key property that matters (`match_id`
uniqueness) passed 4/4 splits. Ruling: assertion at fault, not the frame.
The executor correctly refused to weaken its committed gate mid-run
(`raw/contract.log` preserves the original FAIL); the orchestrator
corrected the harness post-ruling and re-ran:
`raw/contract_after_ruling.log` → `I19 CONTRACT GATE: PASS`.

## Surfaced finding (strengthens I19's motivation)

Because the frozen `data/xgb_match_data_i7` keys `match_id` by the display
string, **the successor line's frame of record has a non-unique primary key
on 45 rows** (train 29 / val 6 / test 10 lost keys; +5 in the withheld
golden pool). Any legacy-`match_id` join against it silently fans out or
picks a winner on those rows. The v2 frame removes this (798/798, 7972/7972
unique). I17's own eval chain was unaffected — it joined via the
`cricsheet_id` column — but the hazard was real for any future consumer.

Secondary deliverable: the i19 model dirs are stamped
`cricsheet_primary_v1` where the i17 arms carry `synthetic_fixture_v1` —
same weights (byte-identical pickles), honest provenance.
