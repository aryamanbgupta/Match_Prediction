# A13 — Sim dispersion calibration on sampled score totals

**Verdict: SUPERSEDED-by-D16** (2026-08-03, STEP 0 pre-check; no eval burned,
no implementation, nothing to revert).

## Hypothesis

The sim under-disperses sampled score totals (idea-text premise, legacy
balanced-weights stack: `team_first_over` P10–P90 coverage 53% vs ideal 80%,
`batter_runs` 72%; B5/B15 quote layer cp15 0.664). Under-dispersion is a
variance defect orthogonal to marginal calibration and inflates over/under
tail Brier; fitting per-family dispersion scalars on val to widen the spread
to nominal coverage was the proposed fix.

STEP 0 before fitting anything: re-measure P10–P90 coverage on the PROMOTED

(`research/handoff/A13/raw/coverage_recompute.txt`). The recompute reproduces

sim, or eval was run; no code was changed.

## Verdict logic

On the promoted stack, ALL five under-dispersion families are inside the
pre-committed [0.70, 0.90] band (min 73.56%); the gross defect the idea was
built on is gone (premise-era 64.6% on first-over → 77.2%). The paired twin
control (same i7 frame, same seed, balanced weights + vector calibrator,
68.97–77.23%) attributes most of the movement to the no-weights retrain
itself — uniform-weight training widened the sampled-total spread, exactly
the mechanism the supervisor flagged as plausible. The sole out-of-band
family, `batter_fours` at 91.37%, is OVER-dispersed: A13's widening method
would push it further out. STEP 0 rule fires → SUPERSEDED-by-D16.
