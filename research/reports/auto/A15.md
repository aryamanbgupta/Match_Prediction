# A15 — Minimal over-0-only ball calibrator (A14 parsimony follow-up)

**Verdict: LANDED** (sim gate pair) — 2026-07-11
**Commit (implementation):** `d6202e0`

## Hypothesis

A14 LANDED a 20-vector per-over ball calibrator (`OverVectorScalingCalibrator`)
that improved `team_first_over_mae` (dMAE −0.022, CI excluding 0) over the E5 v1
single global vector. A14's own diagnostic showed the *entire* first-over win is
concentrated in **over 0** — its vector diverges most from global (six ×1.23,
wicket ×1.23), while the other 19 per-over vectors buy nothing observable and are
pure overfitting surface. A **2-vector** calibrator (one over-0 vector + the
global vector everywhere else) should capture the full first-over gain with 10×
fewer parameters — more robust, and cleanly isolating *where* the sim's first-over
error lives.

## What I did

- `scripts/auto/a15_fit_over0_calibrator.py`: refit the calibrator from the same
  validation balls A14/v1 used (venue_encoded = 0), keeping **only over 0** in
  `_v` plus the global fallback. Overs 1–19 have no entry, so
  `OverVectorScalingCalibrator._vector_for` falls back to `_global` for them → the
  sim behaves EXACTLY like the E5 v1 single-vector calibrator everywhere except
  over 0.
- **Byte-identity verified** at fit time (max abs diff = 0.00e+00 on all three):
  - `_global` == E5 v1 vector,
  - A15 over-0 vector == A14 over-0 vector,
  - A15 `_global` == A14 `_global`.
  So the over-0 correction is byte-identical to A14; A15 only drops overs 1–19 →
  global. This isolates the parsimony question exactly.
- Artifact `models/auto/a15/over0_calibrator.pkl` (2 effective vectors vs A14's
  20; gitignored, reproducible via the fit script).
- Recipe B: `prop_backtest.py --n-matches all --n-sims 100 --seed 42
  --ball-calibrator vector --ball-calibrator-path
  models/auto/a15/over0_calibrator.pkl` → `detail_over0_n261.json` (2223 s).
- Paired gate via `scripts/auto/a8_gate_analysis.py` vs the SAME single-vector
  baseline A14 used, `models/auto/a8/detail_vec_n261.json`
  (cluster-bootstrap by match, seed 29).

No eval-framework edit; no production-default change (the default `vector` path
still loads `vector_scaling_calibrator_v1.pkl`, byte-unchanged). Sim engine
already dispatches `over` to the calibrator via A14's landed wiring.

## Verdict

**LANDED.** The entire `team_first_over_mae` win A14 delivered is carried by the
**over-0 vector alone**; the other 19 per-over vectors are inert (or slightly
overfit). A 2-vector calibrator (over-0 + global) retains the full gain
(dMAE −0.018, CI [−0.032, −0.003], excludes 0) with 10× fewer parameters and no
guard regression. A15 is the recommended minimal first-over ball calibrator,
superseding A14's 20-vector artifact for that purpose; A14 stays LANDED (it
established the win, A15 refines it to the minimal robust form). Commits kept.

## What I'd try next

The over-0 miscalibration was also inflating the powerplay-total prop
(`pp_total_ou_45_5` improves −0.0037, CI excludes 0, once over 0 is fixed) — the
sim's raw over-0 marginal (six/wicket tilted ~1.23×) is the single largest
per-over calibration defect. A natural next probe: are there a *small* number of
other "regime-change" overs where the sim's marginal is comparably off — e.g.
over 6 (start of the middle, new bowler after the field restriction) or over 15
(start of the death) — that would each earn their own vector on the same
single-over logic, while the remaining overs keep global? That keeps the A15
parsimony discipline (only add a vector where it's mechanically distinct and
observable) instead of A14's blanket 20-vector grid. Lower priority than A13's
dispersion lever, which attacks the tail-overshoot props A8/A14/A15 (all
marginal-rate correctors) structurally cannot move.

A10 (sim-P(win) as a *direct-model feature*) is the higher-listed PENDING P3
idea, but its "~1 h, envelope already exists" budget rests on a false premise:
the only sim envelope on disk (`eval_out_phase5_hier/hier_all_20260425_165622.json`)

compute (261 matches = ~37 min), far beyond one iteration. A10 as written is not
runnable in a single night without a full train-set sim pass; it needs
re-scoping (e.g. generate a cached train-set sim envelope as a separate,
multi-night data task) before it can be evaluated. Left untouched in the queue.
