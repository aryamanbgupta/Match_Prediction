## Why the primary didn't move (mechanism, found pre-run by the unit check)

The weight table showed defect (a) fixed as intended — true debutants
1.153% → 8.733% full-XI share (actual ≈9%) — but defect (b) moved the
**wrong way**: veteran never-bowlers (≥20 appearances, 0 career balls)
0.270% → 0.496% (ratio 1.83). B9's exp_balls shrinks a 0-ball veteran to
k_u·prior/(k_u+n) ≈ 1–2 balls at k_u=5, which is *above* the legacy α
share. The B9 usage baseline itself never fixed (b) (it prices that
cohort 1.30% vs sim 1.29%; actual seen-never-took-wicket top rate 0.62%)
— its CI-clean win came from (a) plus the head. So B10-as-specified
attacks (a) only, and (a)'s effect shows up exactly where it should:
debutant-driven wicket families (`bowler_wkts_1plus` — the residual
overshoot D15 left at +0.0047 [−0.0001, +0.0095] — plus early-wicket
lines), while `top_bowler` nets ~flat because (b) worsened.
